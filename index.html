How a visit works

Three steps, and you never have to carry a bike anywhere.

  1. 1

    Book a time

    Pick the repair and a two-hour window. We confirm by text the same day.

  2. 2

    We roll up

    The van parks nearby with a full stand, tools and common parts on board.

  3. 3

    Ride away

    Test the bike with the mechanic, then pay by card or phone. No deposit.

What we promise every rider

  • The price we quote is the price you pay

    If a repair needs more than we expected, we stop and ask before doing anything extra.

  • A 30-day ride guarantee

    If the same fix comes loose within 30 days, we come back and redo it for free.

  • Tidy work, wherever we park

    Drop cloth under the stand, old parts taken away for recycling, nothing left on your driveway.

  • Straight answers

    If a bike is not worth fixing, we say so and tell you why, even if it costs us the job.

Questions riders ask

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the booking form.

Where do you need to park?

Any spot within about 50 feet of the bike works. A driveway, a loading bay or a street space is fine. The stand runs on battery, so we do not need power.

Do I need to be there?

Only for the start and the test ride. Many riders leave the bike with a front desk or in a garage and we text a photo when it is done.

What if you need a part you don’t carry?

We tell you the price, order it, and come back for the second visit at no extra travel charge.

Which bikes do you work on?

Road, city, mountain, kids’ bikes, cargo bikes and most e-bikes. For e-bike motors and batteries we do checks and simple fixes, not internal repairs.

How far do you travel?

Anywhere inside the city limits at no extra cost. Outside that, ask us in the form and we will quote the trip.
